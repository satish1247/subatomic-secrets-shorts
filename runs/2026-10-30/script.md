Notes on facts
- Provisional designation 2026 ED138 from the IAU Minor Planet Center (announced Oct 27, 2026); NSF–DOE Vera C. Rubin Observatory's first dwarf planet candidate (NSF, NOIRLab, UW, SLAC).
- Matthew Holman spotted it Oct 13 while running Rubin data through moving-object search code; Mario Jurić found 20 more Rubin observations back to March (first Rubin observation 4 Mar 2026, MPC).
- Near the plane of the Milky Way, crowded with millions of stars; Rubin's repeated monitoring revealed its motion (NSF, UW).
- ~46 au now; Pluto averages ~40 au (UW, NSF). Period ~281 yr (MPC record), ~282.7 yr (Wikipedia orbit table) -> "roughly 280 years".
- Size estimate 600–1,500 km from distance, brightness and assumed reflectivity (UW, CNN, Forbes); Pluto 2,377 km (NASA via coverage).
- Five IAU dwarf planets: Pluto, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, Makemake; it is only a candidate until confirmed (SciAm, CNN, UW).
- Not used (unclear/single source): "30 images across 20 nights", DECam archival images back to 2016, "biggest in 20 years".
- Animation is schematic, not to scale (except the Pluto vs ED138 size panel, drawn to scale).
