Notes on facts
- Launched November 2004, two-year primary mission, ran nearly 22 years; final observation Oct 20 2026; NASA announced end Oct 23 (NASA, Live Science, Technology.org, AIAA, The Register).
- Safe mode when it could no longer compensate for extra drag from an upper atmosphere expanded by strong solar activity; Swift has no onboard propulsion (NASA, Live Science, The Register, NewsBytes).
- Katalyst LINK (launched July 3 on Pegasus XL) lost reaction wheels / thruster function, spun, used propellant; Aug 19 NASA/Katalyst dropped the boost attempt (CNN, Space.com, EarthSky). Script says only "ran into trouble" (outlets disagree on attitude-control vs propulsion).
- >2,000 GRBs, >1,400 supernovae; ~12 TB public archive (Live Science, Martin Cid, PTTL, NASA).
- Slewing to bursts in about a minute (Live Science).
- Re-entry date: NASA says within weeks, no exact date; not stated beyond that.
- Not used (single source): ~7,000 papers; 1.5 million images; 275 km threshold.
