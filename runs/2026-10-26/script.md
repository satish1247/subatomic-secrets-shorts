Notes on facts
- Paper: "Locomotor kinematics and head stabilization in rolling snakes", Current Biology, Oct 19 2026; lead Yosuke Kojima, Toho University (Japan).
- 13 dwarf reedsnakes (genus Pseudorabdion), three species, recorded in Borneo (Sarawak) in 2019 and 2022; multi-angle high-speed video; detailed measurements in the lab.
- Peak rolling speed 1.72–3.69 m/s (3.8–8.3 mph); "3.7 m/s" = 3.69 rounded; ~4x peak lateral-undulation speed (Kojima: on average four times).
- Mechanism: body doesn't rotate as one unit; one side whips forward toward the ground in a "stroke", alternating left and right.
- Head stays relatively steady, facing forward at a nearly constant height.
- Rolled along a 20 cm (7.9 in) wide racetrack.
- Not used: PopSci's "one foot per second" (inconsistent with other coverage); exact species names (outlets differ).
- Animation is schematic (not to scale).
