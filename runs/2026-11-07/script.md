Notes on facts
- First close-up from ~60 km (37 mi), Asteroid Framing Camera, released ~Nov 5 2026 (ESA via Gizmodo, Starlust, EarthSky, TechTimes; OHB release Nov 6).
- Shows the side of Didymos DART never saw; equatorial ridge, boulders, craters, groove/ridge patterns (PI Patrick Michel); some features may be debris from Dimorphos after the impact (Gizmodo, Starlust, EarthSky).
- DART impact 26 Sep 2022; Dimorphos orbit 11 h 55 min -> 11 h 23 min, ~32 min shorter (NASA, Oct 11 2022 briefing; Science News, Space.com, Down To Earth).
- Sizes ~780 m and ~151 m (Starlust; Wikipedia Dimorphos).
- "Crash site investigation" is ESA's phrasing; Hera is ESA's planetary defence mission.
- Not used (unclear): exact arrival date (Nov 5 vs ESA planning), CubeSat release timing, Nov 25 imagery date.
- Animation is schematic, not a real image; size panel drawn to scale.
